# Design decisions

Use content, audience and existing brand to choose composition. Three concepts must change opening geometry and hierarchy, not just palette. Choose one signature that explains actual content. Reusable controls support reliability; ready-made heroes are not substitutes for art direction.

Theme changes alter tokens; a different concept changes composition. Preserve real facts through both. Alternate reading density, scale and section rhythm according to the material. Omit unsupported sections and empty visual boxes. Avoid invented metrics, logos, endorsements and decorative dashboards. An expressive brief deserves a content-specific spatial or interactive idea; a quiet brief deserves restraint.
